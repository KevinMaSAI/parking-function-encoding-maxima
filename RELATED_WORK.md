# Problem and related work

The target is Problem 54 in Ari Cruz et al., *On Some Discrete Statistics of Parking Functions*, Journal of Integer Sequences 27 (2024), Article 24.8.6:

- [Official article](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL27/Harris/harris13.html)
- [Official PDF](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/journals/JIS/VOL27/Harris/harris13.pdf)
- [2023 arXiv first version](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.16786v1)

The alternating class counts predate this manuscript: [A260694](https://oeis.org/A260694) and [A264963](https://oeis.org/A264963) were initiated in 2015. Those numerical counts are not presented as new.

For the classical permutation descent-class maximum, see Sagan, Yeh and Ziegler, *Maximizing Möbius functions on subsets of Boolean algebras*, Discrete Mathematics 126 (1994), 293–311, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/0012-365X(94)90273-9).

Per Alexandersson, *Parking functions, Smirnov words, and noncrossing Chow polynomials*, [arXiv:2609.05131v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.05131v1), gives recurrences for aggregate peak and tie statistics. Its Remark 3.5 explains a boundary of the symmetric-content transfer. The complete encoding comparison here is proved through explicit injections.

This note identifies the references checked during development. It does not claim an exhaustive literature survey or certified historical priority.
