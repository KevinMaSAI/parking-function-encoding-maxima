# AI assistance

OpenAI Codex assisted extensively with mathematical exploration, candidate injections, proof development and checking, exact enumeration, programming, literature screening, English exposition, and repository preparation.

The repository is an AI-assisted research preprint under Qiyuan Ma's name. It records the displayed arguments and executable artifacts. Publication of the repository does not assert that an independent expert has reviewed the proof, that a proof assistant has formalised it, or that a literature search certifies absolute historical priority.

The verification scripts distinguish bounded implementation checks from the general mathematical arguments in the manuscript.
